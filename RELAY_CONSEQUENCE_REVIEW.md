## What was integrated

| Work | Origin → destination | Base | Consequence |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Receiver mounting stock | Market → Works | 120 | A fitted receiving frame appears at Relay; Works offers the assembled receiver. |
| Assembled receiver | Works → Relay | 140 | Paired receiver panels and a steady warm Dispatch header activate; Relay offers outbound work. |
| Market return pouch | Relay → Market | 100 | Repeatable ordinary freight from the new departure point. |

These jobs have no optional objective or mastery slot. Every valid delivery,
including zero-condition cargo, pays the authored base. The two specific job IDs
alone contribute. Unrelated work does not substitute. Replays pay ordinary base
and explicitly say the need is already received; no repeat installation occurs.
Nothing claims the cargo is destroyed. There is no timer or construction process.

Market has the relevant project card plus the five ordinary jobs, browsed three
at a time. Works has one narrowly scoped receiver pickup. Relay has one outbound
card after commissioning. Continue retains the exact reached pose/velocity;
nearby interaction and map guidance use the existing destination-pad data.
There is no arbitrary pickup framework or movement restriction.

## State and reset

One local `user://relay_annex_project_v01.json` contains a format identifier and
exactly two booleans, `fabrication_stock` and `dispatch_receiver`. Receiver without
stock is invalid. Stage 0/1/2, world visibility and outbound availability are
derived, never independently saved. Native user data is isolated under
`Hush Basin Relay Experiment v01`; browser storage belongs to its origin/profile.
Credits, cargo liner, delivery receipts and the four challenge seals remain
session-only. The craft starts at Market after an application restart.

Validated same-directory temporary write and atomic replacement precede in-memory
advancement. Invalid files block contributions rather than silently discarding
state. I/O failure is visible on the board/receipt; payment does not falsely claim
installation. **Reset Project Experiment…** explicitly confirms and resets both
flags, derived geometry and Dispatch. It leaves session rewards intact.

The optional `--control` launcher flag keeps the identical legs, payouts, saved
availability and outbound convenience, with neutral shipment copy and no annex
geometry. Reset before a fresh tester uses it. It shares the experiment save;
there are no profiles or extra save slots. It is not an additional product mode.

## Separate presentation and mastery decisions

The Relay addition is presentation-only: 24 native box instances in three batches
and one label, all inside the existing mast plot, x ±4.475 m, y 12.61–22.24 m,
z −285.45…−281.65 m. Stage changes briefly refresh the particle-only heightfield,
then restore its existing static update mode. No route/collision changes.

South/west approaches and the pad show the receiver silhouette. The east sweep
faces away from the mast; the change can be missed. No camera intervention masks
that weakness. Native captures show the states; they do not establish that a
human notices or remembers them naturally.

Default trail polish is an independent lane: shared ribbon seams, feathered
edges, tapered fade and no bridging across separate emission intervals. One
native material/surface, at most 18 samples and 408 vertices. Meaningful drift
still uses the frozen v1.1 predicate. The original central propulsion particles
remain. The default trail is immediately available; no purchase or mastery gate.
Historical cosmetic resources remain only as comparison/reference source.

Freight seals is now a basic Depot delivery: +80, no optional +60 and no mastery.
Its prior 3.5-second drift required going beyond the natural short route; the
retained example's 20.48 seconds versus 9.23-second direct trip illustrates that
mismatch. Relay Sweep, Works Care, Clinic Thread and Quarry Haul are byte-identical
contract definitions. No thresholds or BRRR/drift predicates were retuned.
Maximum-time evidence rounds upward to hundredths; minimum-duration evidence
rounds downward. A displayed success cannot hide a failure through rounding.
