# District Zero P1A — Terrain Polish R6 owner review

Double-click `PLAY_DISTRICT_ZERO_TERRAIN_POLISH_R6.command` to play.

R6 retains the complete Polish R5 presentation and applies one practical
render-only terrain touch-up. Ordinary terrain classes now blend by `0.22`
across their immediate four-neighbor boundaries. This softens road, shoulder,
bank, and base-color stair steps without blurring same-class interiors.
Gate-band and hard-obstacle colors remain exact.

Terrain heights, surface-class bytes, geometry, collision, physics, movement,
walls, routes, map data, camera, lighting, and shadows are unchanged. This is
an owner-review build, not P1A PASS or Human World Gate authorization.
