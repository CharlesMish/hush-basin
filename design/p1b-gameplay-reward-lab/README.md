# District Zero P1B Gameplay & Reward Design Lab

Status: `COMPLETE — FINAL RECOMMENDATION FROZEN`

This lab explores the first real gameplay layer for District Zero: courier
contracts whose risk/reward amplifies the existing momentum, long-drift, Hop,
and route-learning play. It is deliberately separate from the verified R7
player and does not modify product source.

## Overnight method

Eight hourly iterations each contribute one paint family, one trail direction,
and one evidence-backed refinement to gameplay or reward design. The exercise
is divergent early and convergent late. Options may be rejected; quantity is
not the goal.

The final recommendation must specify:

- the complete player-facing loop and every transition;
- initial contract set and modifiers;
- cargo-integrity, grading, payout, anti-exploit, and progression rules;
- selected launch cosmetics and earning cadence;
- implementation boundaries and deterministic tests;
- tuning ranges and unanswered human-feel questions.

No product gameplay, Human World Gate, publishing, deployment, external asset,
or performance upgrade is authorized in this lab.
