# Foundational Principles

These are the guardrails for the overnight exploration. Numerical values may
change; these principles require an explicit recorded reason to change.

1. **The game pays for expressive driving, not against it.** A clean sustained
   drift is not cargo damage. Only a measured impact episode, rescue/reset, or
   contract-specific hazard can reduce integrity.
2. **Movement remains the toy.** Contracts create reasons to use Drive,
   Spread, braking, Hop, and route knowledge; they do not retune those systems.
3. **Completion always teaches and usually pays.** Ordinary lateness lowers a
   bonus or grade. It does not invalidate delivery. The proof build's active
   reset is the explicit exception: it aborts cleanly and becomes a human-feel
   stop if that erasure discourages retry.
4. **The proof receipt is legible.** Completion, condition, and pace are the
   only first-slice money lines. Grade summarizes the same quality evidence and
   never secretly multiplies the result again.
5. **The odometer cannot mint or dock money.** Base value is authored before a
   run. Actual distance is a receipt fact only; extra loops and alternate valid
   routes cannot change payout.
6. **Routes remain choices.** The three proof jobs are open endpoint contracts.
   Sealed routes remain an explicit later possibility only after human
   time/risk evidence shows an authored route challenge is worth enforcing.
7. **Rewards are expression first.** The proof uses one session Credit balance
   and two paint finalists. Trails, shop, Reputation, and permanent saving wait
   behind the three-job owner gate. Mechanical speed/brake/integrity upgrades
   stay outside the first slice.
8. **The first success changes the craft.** One of two cue-safe proof finishes
   is granted after familiarization, so the reward bridge proves its value
   before it asks for repetition.
9. **No casino or chore architecture.** No random drops, duplicate rewards,
   rotating shop pressure, daily streaks, premium currency, or hidden odds.
10. **Metrics cannot become steering.** Contract and cosmetic systems may
    observe the craft; they may not feed control, velocity, collision, or
    camera behavior back into it.
