## Why modifier combinations remain absent

Do not make a fragile, sealed, timed, multi-stop HOP contract merely because
the words sound game-like. That stack changes cargo response, route validity,
topology, failure pressure, payout class, and likely reference timing at once.
A good or bad reaction would not say which rule caused it.

The exact one-axis signatures therefore reject even a fully rehashed record
that tries to add an unrelated cargo, payout, route, topology, hard-fail, or
distance rule. The settled iteration-04 complexity vocabulary is reused
verbatim. K2 remains deferred.

## Explored alternatives not selected

### Four-job open circuit

The study examined a coherent content-only circuit:

```text
CLN → WRK → RLY → QRY → CLN
```

It could bring L7/R0/X0/HOP/DOG contexts into future authored work without new
failure rules. It is not selected now because four simultaneous definitions
would answer “is more content pleasant?” while delaying the sharper question:
what, specifically, is missing after Gate B? Keep it as a later breadth
backlog, not the first expansion.

### Standard / Express / Fragile launch trio

Rejected for Gate B. It changes route context and cargo/reward emphasis at the
same time. The present C02/C03 names cannot authorize those behaviors.

### Sealed A2 or X0

Deferred. C03 already treats A2 as a legitimate open alternative, and route
entry/direction/re-entry evidence is not yet an explainable product contract.
A route may be sealed only in a later one-axis study after players first say a
featured line is desirable but open incentives consistently bypass it.

### Hard timers, zero-condition failure, recovery bans, and raw-speed upgrades

Rejected for the initial slice. They work against the selected soft-completion
and long-drift learning loop. No raw-speed upgrade enters any challenger.

path spine:

- exactly two vertices per sample; at most 24 samples, 48 vertices, and 46
  triangles;
- sample only after both `1/30 s` and `0.30 m` since the prior sample;
- retain at most `0.46 s` and `15 m`;

  the face;
- a minimum-rotation transported width frame turns exactly once per `12.0 m`
  of private bounded visual-chord history;
- phase begins at zero after every clear, with no RNG;
- depth test on; depth write, shadows, GI, lights, collision, prediction,
  gameplay queries, and per-frame node/material/resource allocation off; and
- pause freezes sampling, expiry, and phase. Reset, restart, scene/profile or
  equip change, invalid/non-finite state, timestamp regression, generic clear,
  or a one-frame displacement `>8 m` clears before rendering.

The chord counter is unsaved, inaccessible to gameplay, and never serves as
distance, route, grade, contract, or payout evidence.

One Wake is distinct from Open Fold: Open Fold is a camera-facing,
three-vertex, center-creased sheet; One Wake is a two-vertex, non-billboarded
transported tape. It has no dual rails, braid, stamps, particles, branch, or
forward projection.

Reduced motion removes twist and uses one quiet camera-facing strand: at most
10 samples, `0.18 s`, `4 m`, fixed `0.024 m` width, linen only, alpha `<=0.14`.

Reject if it resembles navigation or an ideal line, appears ahead, forks,
