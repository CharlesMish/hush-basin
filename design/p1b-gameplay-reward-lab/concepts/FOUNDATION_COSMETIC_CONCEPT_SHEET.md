## Three directions

1. **Foundry Night** — graphite shell, restrained copper structure, twin amber
   ribbon wake.
2. **Polar Signal** — frost shell, deep-teal joints, segmented cyan pulse wake.
3. **Ember Orchid** — plum/charcoal shell, coral panels, sparse split-color
   ember wake.

## What the sheet decided

- Paint should affect shell/panel groups; it should not recolor every material.
- The existing energy state remains authoritative. Cosmetics cannot make
  Spread, Drive, caution, or strike feedback ambiguous.
- A trail belongs behind and below the craft. It must not obscure wall bases,
  route edges, the minimap, or a following camera's view of the vehicle.
- Launch trails should vary in shape and rhythm as well as hue. Pure palette
  swaps alone are not a satisfying long-term reward family.
- Production trails should be substantially narrower and less luminous than
  this deliberately legible concept rendering.
