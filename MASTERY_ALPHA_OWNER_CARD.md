# Play Mastery Alpha v1

Double-click **PLAY_MASTERY_ALPHA.command**. It opens the current candidate at
Market using the installed Godot 4.7.1. Nothing is deployed or saved permanently.

W/S thrust/brake · A/D steer · hold Shift for Drive · Space Hop · R reset · Esc
pause. At Market, E or Enter opens Dispatch. Left/right browses; 1–5 selects a
job directly; Enter accepts. Finish below 6 m/s. Continue leaves you there.
Drive back toward Market's warm rooftop crown when ready.

Try this for 20–30 minutes, in any order:

1. Browse the three visible cards. All five jobs remain available and replayable.
2. Deliver something by an easy route. Base Credits always pay, even at zero
   cargo condition. The optional challenge can wait.
3. Try a mastery: Depot wants a real 3.5-second drift chain. Relay wants the
   curved West Sweep section from after West Gate toward Relay in 10 seconds;
   its section clock is separate from the delivery clock.
4. Try a different kind: Works wants 85% cargo; Clinic wants the clean South Cut
   in Spread via Hop or dogleg; Quarry wants East Sweep northbound and then a
   wall-contact-free Quarry Shelf crossing toward Quarry. Haul has no time or
   form requirement. Choose a form because it helps you drive the road.
5. A first optional success earns that job's seal. One Depot/Relay seal plus
   one Works/Clinic/Quarry seal unlocks the **Lantern amber trail**. Buy it at
   Dispatch for 200 Credits with T or the button; T switches freely afterward.
   The existing 400-Credit cargo liner is independent. Both are session-only.

Look for whether the board is calmer, the seals make a missed bonus worth
revisiting, and the little reward creates a reason for one more run. Is the
amber trail desirable while still clearly communicating real drift? Do the
Sweep start and Shelf retry rules make sense without studying a HUD? Is the
Shelf's strict no-wall-touch rule satisfying or brittle?

F3 bookmarks a surprising contact or line state; F2 opens the retained cargo
evidence. Logs stay locally in `review_logs/`. No cargo tuning was changed.

Tell Astra which jobs you favor, whether Credits or mastery matter more, and
whether this feels cumulative after several deliveries. No new work tier,
obstacles, paints, container, saving or handling upgrade is included. Closing
the app resets Credits, seals and purchases. Long-term motivation across days
is still outside this session experiment.
