## Findings sent to the integration lead

1. Project Results printed `contract.reaction` unconditionally on a delivered
   parcel. A failed save could therefore also claim that the mount was fitted or
   receiver commissioned; replay could incorrectly narrate first-time progress.
   Consequence text must follow the actual contribution status/stage, including
   neutral copy, and must not claim installation when persistence failed.
2. The inherited courier `_input` handles Enter/E/Esc before GUI handling. Guard
   courier input while the Reset confirmation dialog is visible, so confirming
   or canceling the dialog cannot accidentally accept a job or close Dispatch.
3. The single-card Works/Relay board hides both side cards, collapsing the HBox
   to the left. Center the row to retain the selected-card presentation.
4. Neutral control kept named project/annex wording in Reset and completed-job
   detail. Replace it with ordinary shipment/availability wording in that control.

The lead owns disposition and final integrated verification of these findings.
They were found before complete integrated verification, not after its repair
budget began.
