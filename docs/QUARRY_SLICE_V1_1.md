# Hush Basin Chapter 2: Quarry Slice Draft

Scene draft · Version 1.1 · Written to Hush_Basin_Story_Brief_v1_0 · 29 September 2026

**The object.** The working noun is **threshold**. It is a long, flat stone slab set across a doorway, about a door's width. The old one is grey and dished in the middle from years of feet. Two pale wheel tracks are worn across it, and a raised lip runs along its door side. Clinic's current service cart has small wheels that catch on that lip. The new one is pale and flat, with no lip and a shallow bevel on its approach edge, so the cart rolls straight on.

Encounters are listed in play order. The paid stone legs are encounters 1–2 (outward) and 5 (return). Encounters 4 and 6 happen on ordinary work. Nell's tray sits in the interval between.

## 1. Quarry: meeting B

**Ticket** (Quarry Stores, job ticket, mandatory):
> Collection at Quarry Stores: one stone threshold for Clinic's receiving entrance. Ask for B.

**Sees on arrival.** The Stores shed stands with its door open and a coil of rope on a hook beside it. At the doorway, barrow wheels have worn a muddy rut through the gravel. The new threshold rests on a pallet in the yard under the patched tarp, which is folded halfway back to show a pale, honed face. Bea is at the pallet.

**Bea** (portrait, on arrival, mandatory):
> "You'll be the courier. I'm B. Tarp's holding. Those patches'll do."
> "You've come working. We've put the kettle on anyway."

**Bea** (portrait, at release, mandatory):
> "Finished face on the pad. Let them put the first marks on it."
> "There's a note for Clinic about the old one. If they've no use for it, we'd like it back. If it comes, don't fuss. Everything's already happened to that one."

**Release slip** (cargo docket, travels with the stone, mandatory):
> QUARRY STORES · One threshold for Clinic receiving entrance, finished face protected. If Clinic has no further use for the old threshold, please release it to Quarry. —B

**After loading.** The pallet is bare, with the tarp folded on it.

**State.** This becomes available after the opening coda's B receipt. The route runs down the Quarry approach and across the city. South Cut is an expressive option where the map supports it, and the street route stays valid.

## 2. Clinic: the new threshold arrives

**Sees on arrival.** The old threshold lies across Clinic's receiving doorway. It is grey and dished, with its two pale wheel tracks and the raised lip on the door side. The service cart waits just outside with its small front wheels against the lip.

**Clinic Receiving** (receipt, mandatory):
> Received: one threshold for the receiving entrance. It'll be in before the cart's next round. We've seen B's note.

**After completion.** The new threshold lies on its pad beside the door.

**State.** The outward job completes and pays normally. Any existing finish reward applies as usual, and the receipt reads correctly whatever the stone's condition. The crew installs it off-screen; the doorway's visual change happens only while the player is away.

## 3. Market: Nell's first tray

This job becomes available from Relay after encounter 2 and stays available until completed. Its preferred placement is in the interval before encounter 4, and it should not be the job that directly follows encounter 1.

**Ticket** (Ren, Relay, mandatory):
> Empty meal tray to Nell at the Market hatch. Use the return crate below the receiving shelf.

**Sees on arrival.** The hatch is open for service. A wrapped order with a note tucked under its string sits on the receiving shelf, and the return crate is underneath. Behind Nell, a baking tin is mostly portioned out. One dark-edged corner piece sits on its own saucer at her elbow.

**Nell** (portrait, after the tray goes in the crate, mandatory; for the second line she reads the note on the wrapped order):
> "Don't look at the corner. That one's mine. I got here first."
> "'Extra greens.' I've been picking the greens off his for a year. He never said a word."

**State.** The job pays normally. The crate persists for every later tray job. The tin, saucer, and wrapped order belong to this visit only; no meal persists.

## 4. Clinic: an ordinary soap delivery

The interval ends on the next ordinary Clinic delivery; its regular soap order is the natural one. That job's normal receipt stays as written, and the release text below is appended to it, so any ordinary Clinic delivery can host this encounter.

**Sees on arrival.** The new threshold sits flush in the doorway, and two faint grey wheel scuffs already cross its pale face. The service cart stands squarely across it, half in the door. The old threshold leans against the wall on two timber offcuts, lip up.

**Clinic Receiving** (appended to the host job's receipt, mandatory):
> Cart's stopped rattling at the door. The old threshold by the wall is released to Quarry; we've no further use for it. Collection posted.

**State.** The installed threshold keeps this state for the rest of the game. The release and the posting of encounter 5 happen at this receipt, and the old piece stays by the wall until it is collected.

## 5. The old threshold goes back

**Ticket** (Clinic Receiving, job ticket, mandatory):
> Collection at Clinic's receiving entrance: one used threshold, released to B at Quarry Stores. Heavy.

**Sees at pickup.** The old threshold is by the wall, where the receipt said it would be.

**Sees on arrival at Quarry.** Bea is at the shed door, with the rut at her feet and the rope on its hook.

**Bea** (portrait, on completion, mandatory):
> "Hollow between the tracks. That's feet, not the cart."
> "Leave it by our door. I've got a place for it."

**Bea** (portrait, optional chip response, only if existing cargo-condition data registers a fresh chip; inserted before "Leave it by our door"):
> "That's a fresh one. It'll be in good company."

**After completion.** The old threshold lies on two blocks beside the shed door, next to the rut.

**State.** This is a separate paid job with normal pay on safe arrival and no pristine-finish reward. The trim-and-bed state switches while the player is away from Quarry.

## 6. Quarry, later

Any later ordinary Quarry job brings the player back. The natural host is Works' regular return of sharpened chisels, and Bea's line below belongs to that job. If another ordinary job hosts the sighting, its normal receipt stands and the staging carries the encounter alone.

**Sees on arrival.** The rut at the shed door is gone. The old threshold lies bedded level across the entrance. Along its door side, where the lip was, runs a clean sawn edge, pale against the grey. The dished middle and the two wheel tracks are still there. A barrow stands on it with its wheel resting in one of the old tracks. The rope is on its hook.

**Bea** (portrait, on completing the chisels job):
> "Chisels. Good. On the bench. No—leave them there. I'll take them."

**Bea** (portrait, optional return remark, on later ordinary Quarry arrivals):
> "Barrow found the old groove before I did."

**State.** The bedded threshold, the sawn edge, and the barrow persist as the shed door's normal state. Nothing labels the threshold.

## Implementation note

**Proposed assets and states.** The slice needs Bea's portrait and Nell's standard hatch portrait; her seated finale variant belongs later. Quarry needs the new threshold on a pallet under the patched tarp, then three shed-door states: rope hook and rut, old threshold on blocks, and bedded threshold with sawn edge and barrow. Clinic needs three doorway states: old threshold with the cart against its lip, new one on a pad, and installed with scuffs, cart across it, and the old piece on offcuts. Nell needs the hatch, shelf, crate, tin, corner saucer, and wrapped order. Only installation and trim-and-bed change while the player is away; release and the return job happen at the encounter 4 receipt.

**Needs build inspection.** Check the Clinic entrance footprint and whether the cart can be a static prop in two positions. Quarry needs a shed door readable from its normal approach with a drop point nearby; otherwise use the usual apron and move the stone off-screen. Confirm whether condition data can identify a chip, whether tickets show senders, and whether the soap and chisel jobs exist as ordinary work.

**Departures.** "Ask for B" and a written release slip let the request and Clinic's permission survive skipped dialogue. The barrow rut makes the reuse legible and depends on the shed-door footprint.

**Test first.** Can a player recognize the grey slab at Bea's door from the normal driving view? Test the tracks, hollow, and sawn edge at that distance before adding explanatory dialogue.
