**Writer:** Hush Basin Story Writer / native Grok Bot / run nell-wants-20261006-01 / initial draft

Nell wants to taste someone else's cooking, and she wants only the ends. She wraps her own corner piece, posts it west to Quarry Stores with a hard ask — both heels of their loaf, not the loaf — and when the heels come back she sits at her hatch and gives a short, uneven verdict. Two paid legs, real cargo both ways, no empty return. Bea answers on paper only. Nobody is told how the tasting went.
