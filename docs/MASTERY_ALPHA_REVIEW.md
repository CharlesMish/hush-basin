# Mastery Alpha v1 — integration decision

Accepted source: Mechanics Range V1.1,
`39d098c52b028db3d2b2ca0c41c7c71de89b8791`. The eight direct owner observations
remain in `MASTERY_OWNER_EVIDENCE.md`. No observation about the core toy was
recast as a failure. Three isolated lab branches were reviewed; selected new
modules were copied individually into one successor. No overlapping branch
was blindly merged. No main update, push or deployment occurred.

## What was integrated

- Three visible Dispatch cards, centered selection, deterministic left/right
  wrap through all five jobs, with 1–5 quick selection retained.
- One first optional-success mastery seal per contract. Base delivery remains
  meaningful without mastery. Valid replays pay the same full authored receipt.
- One small session reward: varied mastery unlocks an optional 200-Credit
  Lantern amber trail. The 400-Credit cargo liner is unchanged.
- Relay and Haul observe execution of named existing roads. They no longer
  award a bonus for global Drive/Spread distance counters.
- Results show first mastery/unlock and, for Relay, the actual section time.
  F3 retains cargo/line evidence and adds route/mastery snapshots.

## Lane review and rejections

| Lane | Selected | Rejected or deferred |
|---|---|---|
| Progression | First mastery; one line-family and one service-family seal unlock amber; fixed 200-Credit purchase | Arbitrary 2/4-stamp work gates, locking accepted Thread/Haul, invented harder tier, sixth job, XP, repeat penalties, persistence |
| Challenge audit | Keep Drift Chain, Care and Thread; timed West Sweep; East Sweep then clean Shelf | Raising global mode counters; making every job drift; retuning movement; hidden mode/time rule for Haul |
| Dispatch | Three neighboring cards with centered selection and wrap browsing | Fixed 3+2 pages, full-screen quest log, rotation/scarcity; all five remain immediately accessible |
| Furniture study | Retain existing authored features and ordinary release spaces | Additional pylons/slalom/bar; two distant wall-name plate studies did not establish enough visibility benefit |
| Reward study | RGB-only Lantern amber trail | Paint/container/catalog; no new cosmetic rendering system, no functional feedback advantage |

There is deliberately **no new contract tier**. The lanes did not validate a
distinct new piece of work worth locking behind stamps. Taking away accepted
Thread/Haul would test access frustration more than progression. Mastery → one
visible reward is the smaller honest test. If that fails to motivate, adding
more counters is not assumed to fix it.

## Board before and after

V1.1: five cards in a two-column board, 1120 × 610, with optional labels and a
liner purchase. Candidate: three cards in a 1032 × 492 board; selected card is
always centered. A position indicator and arrows expose the entire pool. Cards
show destination/name, archetype, route character, base/bonus and delivery or
mastery status. Selected instructions are below the cards. Reward explanation
is one row; purchases clearly state that the session resets on exit.

## Contract definitions

| Job | Before optional | After optional | Base + bonus |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Depot / Freight seals | 3.5 s Drive drift, 22+ m/s, 15–75° slip | Unchanged; presented as Drift Chain | 80 + 60 |
| Relay / Longline coil | 170 m clean Drive, 24+ m/s, <=45° slip, anywhere | West Sweep road section within 10 s, after GW junction edge to before RLY junction edge | 100 + 200 |
| Works / Bench instruments | At least 85% condition | Unchanged Care | 120 + 35 |
| Clinic / South desk kit | Clean west-to-east South Cut in Spread, Hop or dogleg | Unchanged Thread | 100 + 300 |
| Quarry / Shelf survey pack | Drive 160 m → Spread 40 m → Drive 90 m | East Sweep northbound, then Quarry Shelf toward Quarry without wall contact; any form, no time limit | 140 + 420 |

Drive remains a traversal form. BRRR remains the same broad expression score.
Drift Chain remains the stricter V1.1 continuous-slip observer with zero grace;
straight Drive cannot extend it. Relay is Express execution of a bend, not a
claim of collision purity: minor contact may still pass within the reference.
Haul is clean-road execution, not forced transformation. A clean all-Spread or
skilled Drive traversal is valid. Only the optional challenge refers to roads;
every legal shortcut still completes the delivery and pays base.

The Sweep clock starts in an 8 m road-entry band after the existing 38 m GW
junction radius, and ends 46 m before RLY. Leaving its 17 m half-width corridor
ends that optional attempt. Return to its entry to retry. A slower completion
records its section time without failing delivery. The target came from 9–10 s
organized runs versus 11.3–24.17 s slower examples, not a guessed global timer.

Haul banks the East approach, then asks for a whole clean Shelf crossing inside
its existing 12 m operational half-width. Shelf contact or road departure
requires restarting that Shelf section from the Relay end; East remains earned.
Retry/fall/diagnostic cancellation returns the parcel and clears all route state
on the next acceptance. Ordinary support counters do not count as wall contact.

## Progression model

The accepted `AlphaSession` Credit ledger remains byte-identical. A small
subclass observes its immutable settled receipt exactly once. Each known
contract stores delivery count and one first optional-success stamp. Failed or
canceled receipts cannot later become mastery; reopening Results cannot pay or
stamp again. No craft telemetry enters progression.

The reward unlock requires one Depot/Relay seal and one Works/Clinic/Quarry
seal. Buying amber debits exactly 200 Credits once, owns it and equips it.
Switching owned palettes is free at Dispatch. The standard trail is fully
functional without any purchase. Price rationale: Depot mastery (140) plus
Thread mastery (400) pays 540; choose amber now, or liner first and one ordinary
Depot delivery to afford amber afterward. Other jobs may reach it sooner; no
grind or repeat penalty was added. The result is an alpha choice, not balance.

Amber changes only RGB roles. Width, alpha, lifetime, sampling, geometry,
qualification and streak response remain unchanged. The standard palette is
exact; sampled linear-luminance deviation is at most 0.694%. Perceptual
desirability is still an owner question.

## Limits to judge

This can test cumulative motivation within one 20–30 minute session. It cannot
test returning days later without future persistence. Care is intentionally an
accessible route to one service seal. Shelf's no-touch rule is stricter than
cargo forgiveness and could feel brittle. Named section entry readability,
10-second human difficulty, repeated return-to-Market travel and cosmetic appeal
need owner judgment. No claim of final challenge balance or economy is made.

Exact verification, calibration, performance and artifact sizes are recorded in
`MASTERY_VERIFICATION.md` and the external review packet. Individual failures
and historical superseded assertions remain visible there.
