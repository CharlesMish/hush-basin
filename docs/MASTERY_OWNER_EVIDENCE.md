# Direct owner evidence — preserve without reinterpretation

The following is Charlie's supplied human evidence for this commission:

1. The five-job version is fun and directionally successful.
2. Thread and Haul are valuable because they finally use previously neglected parts of the city.
3. The five-card Dispatch presentation is getting visually busy.
4. The owner wants more variety over time, but does not want Market to become a giant quest-center menu.
5. The largest conceptual issue is that some challenge language still conflates being in Drive with doing something skillful.
6. Straight high-speed Drive is enjoyable traversal, but it is not inherently a difficult optional objective.
7. A genuine long drift is interesting because the player must manage approach angle, steering, correction, speed and recovery.
8. The game still lacks a meaningful feeling of progression or longer-term motivation beyond the fact that the vehicle itself is fun.

These are the next design problem, not complaints about the core driving toy.
The isolated lane findings and integration decisions are agent interpretations;
they must not be presented as additional owner playtest results.
