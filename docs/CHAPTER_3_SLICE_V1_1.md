# Hush Basin Chapter 3: Slice Draft

Scene draft · Version 1.1 · Written to Hush_Basin_Story_Brief_v1_0, after Quarry Slice v1.1 · 30 September 2026

**Changes in v1.1**, following Astra's review. The sleeve's patch is now an earlier Tess repair, not part of the opening kit. Background character poses are gone; Nell at the interruption is her standard portrait at the handoff. Nell's third line is cut. Bea's reply now answers Tess's question on its own. The reply check is restated as a requirement for these scenes. The chapter's end state is explicit. One refinement goes beyond the review: the sleeve's mark is now a whole new lining in red canvas instead of a stitched stripe, so no ridge touches finished stone and the correct side shows from the normal view.

**The sleeve.** This is a long padded canvas wrap that Quarry lays over finished stone for carrying. Its lining goes against the stone and its outer face stays up. Lining and outer face are the same grey-brown canvas, so from the bench they look alike. One edge carries a neat square patch from an earlier Tess repair, close to the lining. Put down lining-first in the yard, the lining picks up grit and carries it to the next stone.

**Order and legs.** Two threads run through the chapter, the sleeve and Ren's promise. They can interleave freely. The order below is preferred, with ordinary work between each consequential conversation, and every job stays available until completed.

| # | Route | Carries | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Relay → Market | Tray | Budgeted tray job, no dialogue |
| 2 | Quarry → B11 | Sleeve | Paid story job |
| 3 | Relay → Market | Tray | Budgeted tray job |
| 5 | Relay → B11 | Thread, with Ren's note | Required ordinary delivery, hosting a note |

All seven legs count in the chapter budget. The three ordinary deliveries carry required notes or show the new arrangement, so they are not optional padding. Encounters 4 and 9 have no leg of their own; each attaches to an ordinary Relay arrival. Three replies travel on completion receipts to the job's requester, which is the main build check. The sleeve thread ends at encounter 7 and Ren's thread at encounter 9; Chapter 4 opens only when both are complete.

## 1. Market: a busy hatch

**Ticket** (Ren, Relay, mandatory):
> Nell's tray. Return crate below the shelf.

**Sees on arrival.** The hatch is fully up and service is on. Three wrapped orders wait on the receiving shelf, with the crate beneath. On the stool behind the hatch, a saucer holds one dark-edged corner piece, untouched.

**Text.** The standard receipt only. No portrait.

**State.** Available at the start of the chapter. Pays normally. The stool is the same prop encounter 3 uses.

## 2. Quarry → B11: the sleeve goes south

**Ticket** (Quarry Stores, job ticket, mandatory):
> Collection at Quarry Stores: one sleeve for Tess at B11.

**Sees at pickup.** The sleeve lies rolled on the bench with a tag tied through its patched corner. A handling card is pinned above the bench: SLEEVES — PLAIN SIDE TO THE STONE.

**Bea** (portrait, at pickup, mandatory, bound to this job):
> "It's been leaving marks on finished work. That patch sits too near the face for my liking."

**Tag** (Bea, tied to the sleeve, shown at B11 delivery, mandatory):
> New one to this pattern, please. Plain side to the stone. —B

**Sees after completion at B11.** Tess has the sleeve unrolled across her counter.

**Tess** (portrait, on completion, mandatory):
> "She wants a new one because mine shows."
> "Hang on. Grit, right through the lining. And 'plain side to the stone'? They're both plain."

**Tess's receipt** (completion receipt to Quarry, mandatory):
> Sleeve received. Grit in the lining; patch is sound. Has it been going down face-first? I can reline and keep the cloth, or make new. Holding it till you say. —T

**State.** Available at chapter start. The job pays normally, and Tess's receipt posts encounter 6. Until encounter 7 the sleeve hangs on a hook behind B11's counter, tag showing.

## 3. Market: one more for the north crew

**Ticket** (Ren, Relay, mandatory):
> Nell's tray back to Market. Usual return place. She'll do one more for the north crew. Bring it back up with you.

**Sees at pickup.** A folded note is clipped to the tray.

**Sees on arrival.** Service is over and the hatch is partly lowered. The shelf above the crate is empty. Just visible behind the hatch, a plate sits on the stool: a corner piece with one bite gone and the fork laid across it. Nell isn't at the hatch. When the tray goes in the crate, her standard portrait appears at the handoff. No movement is animated.

**Nell** (standard hatch portrait, mandatory):
> "I'd only just sat down."
> "Ren's promised them another? I didn't say that. I've finished."

**Nell's receipt** (completion receipt to Relay, mandatory):
> Tray in. No extra. Nobody asked me. —N

**After completion.** The portrait closes, and the note lies on the shelf.

**State.** Available after encounter 1. The extra meal exists only in the ticket's prose and the objective list shows the tray alone, so there is nothing to withdraw and nothing to fail. The tray pays normally whenever it arrives. The plate and note belong to this visit only.

## 4. Relay: Ren hears back

This attaches to the first Relay arrival after encounter 3, whatever job brings the player there.

**Sees on arrival.** Ren is at the desk by the receiver. A small box of thread with a folded note on top waits for collection.

**Ren** (portrait, mandatory, cargo-neutral):
> "Nell's receipt came in. I told the north crew yes on my way to asking her, and never got to the asking."
> "I've taken it back. They were very nice about it, which was worse."

**State.** This arrival posts encounter 5. No further line concerns the tray.

## 5. Relay → B11: a question in the thread box

**Ticket** (Ren, Relay, mandatory):
> Thread for Tess at B11. There's a note in with it.

**Note** (Ren, in the box, shown at B11 delivery, mandatory):
> Could your shelf take south-end drops when you're busy? Parcels, the odd crate. Say no if it's no. —Ren

**Sees on arrival.** The space in front of B11's counter is clear except for a stool. If Quarry's sleeve is still being held, it hangs behind the counter.

**Tess** (portrait, on completion, mandatory):
> "Mending, yes. Tag it and it goes on the shelf."
> "Crates, no. Out front's for sitting. Last box I left there, someone sat on it for an hour."

**Tess's receipt** (completion receipt to Relay, mandatory):
> Thread received. Shelf: tagged mending yes, freight no. —T

**State.** This posts encounter 8. While the player is away, a label appears on B11's receiving shelf, and it persists.

## 6. Quarry → B11: kneeling pads, and an answer

**Ticket** (Quarry Stores, job ticket, mandatory):
> Collection at Quarry Stores: two kneeling pads for Tess at B11. Straps want stitching.

**Sees at pickup.** The pads lie on the bench with a note tucked under one strap. The handling card above them is unchanged. No portrait text.

**Note** (Bea, tucked in the pads, shown at B11 delivery, mandatory):
> Yes, on the yard while we clear the bench. That side isn't marked. Reline it if you like. —B

**Tess** (portrait, on completion, mandatory):
> "The seam held. That didn't make the lining fit to use."
> "New lining, same cloth, and a face you can spot from the bench."

**State.** Tess's receipt at encounter 2 posts this job, which stays available until completed. The pads are ordinary mending with a normal receipt. Relining happens while the player is away and then posts encounter 7.

## 7. B11 → Quarry: the sleeve goes home

**Ticket** (B11, job ticket, mandatory):
> Collection at B11: Quarry's sleeve, relined. To B at Quarry Stores.

**Sees at pickup.** The sleeve lies folded on the counter. Its new lining is red canvas, showing at the fold against the old grey-brown outer cloth. The hook behind the counter is empty.

**Tag** (Tess, tied to the sleeve, mandatory):
> Relined. Cloth's got years in it. Red side to the stone. —T

**Sees on arrival.** Bea is at the bench, below the handling card.

**Bea** (portrait, on completion, mandatory):
> "Well. Nobody's going to miss that."
> "It's good. I'd still have bought a new one. Less to check."

**After completion.** The card has been corrected in pencil. PLAIN is struck through with RED written above it, and a new line reads NEVER DOWN ON ITS FACE.

**Bea** (portrait, optional return remark, on later ordinary Quarry arrivals):
> "Crew keeps showing me the red side. I've seen the red side."

**State.** The job pays normally. The corrected card and the sleeve lying red side up on the bench persist on later Quarry arrivals. This completes the sleeve thread.

## 8. Relay → B11: first thing on the shelf

**State.** Available after encounter 5. The cargo is one ordinary tagged mending parcel routed through Relay. The shelf label persists.

## 9. Relay: a letter from Stop 9

This attaches to the first Relay arrival after encounter 8 that carries no other Ren line.

**Ren** (portrait, mandatory, cargo-neutral):

## Implementation note

**Proposed assets and states.** The Market scenes need Nell's standard hatch portrait and a meal setting behind the partly lowered hatch: stool, saucer, plate, and fork. The sleeve needs two states, grey-brown on both faces with the patch, then relined in red. B11 needs a hook for the held sleeve and the shelf label. Quarry needs the handling card, plain and corrected. The thread box, pads, and tagged parcel are ordinary cargo. Relay's pinned letter is optional; the line works without it. No background character poses are assumed.

**Needs build inspection.** For each of the three replies, can the completion present the attributed reply and make the requester's later response available through an established communication channel? If not, choose a supported route during implementation planning rather than adding courier trips. Also check that Quarry → B11 and Relay → B11 ordinary jobs exist or can be posted, that the card, label, and red lining read from the normal view, and that a ticket can mention the extra meal without the objective system expecting a pickup.

**Production clarification.** The mark is the whole new lining in red canvas, flat on its working face, so nothing rough meets finished stone.

**Departures.** The extra meal is ticket prose only. Bea's pickup line makes her concern practical before Tess misreads it. Tess offers relining or new work, and Bea permits relining. Quarry's reply rides a specific ordinary job. The corrected card is the visible changed instruction.
