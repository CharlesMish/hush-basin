# Hush Basin — three-job alpha

Double-click **PLAY_COURIER_ALPHA.command**. You start at Market with 0 Credits.
The warm rooftop crown marks Market Hall; the small map cue points to the actual
Dispatch pad. It is visible from much of the district, not through every building.

**E / Enter / B** opens Dispatch when supported and slow on Market's inner pad.
Click a card, press **1 / 2 / 3**, or use left/right (D-pad supported), then
**Enter / B** to accept. Each job stays available for replay. Map routes are
suggestions. Every legal shortcut counts.

- **Depot — Freight seals:** 100 base, +25 for one 0.4-second BRRR streak.
- **Relay — Signal spares:** 150 base, +40 within 55 active seconds.
- **Works — Bench instruments:** 120 base, +35 at 75% cargo or better.

Missing a bonus is fine. Delivering always pays the base, even with zero cargo.
Settle on the destination's inner pad below 6 m/s. Results show the full receipt.
Continue leaves you there. Drive home to Market when you want the next job.

At **400 Credits**, buy the **cargo liner** on Dispatch (button or **C**). It
reduces accepted cargo-loss bills by 25%. It never changes handling, impact
response or raw telemetry. There is exactly one purchase. Balance and liner
reset when the app closes; no permanent progression is promised.

Movement is unchanged: W/S thrust/brake, A/D steer, hold Shift Drive, release
Spread, Space supported Hop. Escape pauses. R resets and returns an active
parcel without a payout; it never teleports you home after success.
Run v0 remains separately available through **PLAY_RUN_V0.command**.

**F2** shows/hides the retained cargo receipt overlay. **F3** bookmarks a
surprising contact. The normal alpha launcher records local JSONL files in
`review_logs/alpha-*.jsonl`; the launcher prints the path. The overlay/log can
show raw loss → liner reduction → committed loss. Please keep a log if a new
reproducible cargo defect appears; accepted v1.1 tuning is otherwise closed.

## A short owner play

Try all three jobs, including at least one missed bonus. Drive back normally.
Repeat a favorite to afford the liner, buy it, then make another delivery.
Try expressive driving naturally rather than performing a score audit.

1. Does choosing jobs make Market feel like a meaningful home?
2. Do the jobs lead to different driving styles?
3. Do Express / Care / Style encourage fun, or make driving feel managed?
4. Is missing a bonus still fine?
5. Do Credits make another run more appealing?
6. Is the liner rewarding without making cargo irrelevant?
7. Is returning toward Market's crown pleasant, or a chore?
8. After several deliveries, do you want more content?

This candidate is local only. Production was not deployed. Stop at this loop;
no narrative, saving, additional contracts, upgrades or city polish are included.
