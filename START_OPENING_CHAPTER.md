# Hush Basin — Opening Chapter v0.1

Double-click **PLAY_OPENING_CHAPTER.command**.

This is one connected chapter. Its first launch starts fresh and preserves the
older lab saves. To start again, open Dispatch and choose **Reset Opening
Chapter…**, then confirm. If you reset away from Market, drive back there to
collect the first parcel.

W/S thrust/brake · A/D steer · hold Shift for Drive · Space Hop.
E/Enter interact · Enter advance · Left back · X skip · Escape close/pause.
Work always needs a separate acceptance. You can leave an offer waiting.

Play first; no design reading is needed. There is no required reading time or
delivery deadline. Ordinary work remains available between the character jobs.

After playing:

- Tell me about the people you met.
- Where does each of them work?
- Did anything change about your craft?
- Is there anyone you’d go back to see?
- Anything you’re curious about?
- Do you want to keep driving?
