# Hush Basin

Double-click `PLAY_HUSH_BASIN_STORY.command`.

The opening and both following chapters play in one continuous save.
To start again, stop at a desk, open Dispatch, and choose **Reset Story**.
Earlier review saves are preserved.

W/S thrust and brake · A/D steer · hold Shift for Drive · Space Hop.
E / Enter interact · Enter advance · Left review the previous panel.
X skips to the handoff summary. Work always needs a separate acceptance.
Esc closes a conversation or pauses. R resets the craft and returns active cargo.

Portraits and small physical props are temporary prototype art.
