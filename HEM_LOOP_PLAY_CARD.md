# Hem Loop · play card

The Depot freight crew's old turning loop, on a wet evening. One circuit, one
short story. Nothing is saved; your story save and the usual game are untouched.

**Start**
- Browser: unzip `hem_loop_web.zip`, double-click `PLAY_IN_BROWSER.command`
  (or run `python3 serve_hem_loop.py`), then click the page once.
- From this branch: `python3 tools/play_rink.py` (exact Godot 4.7.1;
  set `GODOT_BIN` if needed), or double-click `PLAY_RINK.command` on a Mac.

**Keys** · W/S thrust & brake · A/D steer · hold **Shift = Drive** (fast,
slides) · release = **Spread** (grips, brakes hard, can Hop) · **Space** Hop ·
**R** back to the grid (a fresh attempt) · **Esc** pause, then **T** time trial
or **C** crew run. Title card: **W** or **Enter** races, **C** starts the crew run.
Gamepad: left stick, A = Drive, Y = Hop, X = grid, Start = pause, Back = crew run.

The clock starts when you cross the line. Every lap after that is a flying lap:
your time, the delta to your best and four sector splits appear as you cross.

## Try these two things

1. **Race it, then retake the Hem.** Take one lap however it comes. Next lap,
   at the boards before the big left-hander, brake (S) — or let go of Shift for
   a blink as you turn in — then hold Drive, full left and throttle, and let it
   hang sideways around the turned-up outer edge. Watch the Hem split go green.
   Bonus: the red **HOP** buffer at Crane Corner. Spread, Space just before it,
   glide across the dock.
2. **Crew run (Esc → C).** Tess is on the inside of the Hem, holding a jacket
   over the wall. The fast line you just learned sails right past her. Unfold
   early, brush the inside wall, take it without stopping, and bring it back
   to the line clean. The night crew will tell you why that bend has a name.
