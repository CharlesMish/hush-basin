# Mechanics Range V1.1 — owner review

Double-click **PLAY_MECHANICS_V1_1.command**. It uses the already installed exact
Godot 4.7.1 engine, prepares the project, and starts at Market. This is a local,
session-only candidate. No production deployment or main update.

W/S thrust/brake; A/D steer; hold Shift for Drive; release for Spread; Space Hop.
At Market, E opens Dispatch. Choose 1–5, then Enter. Esc pauses; R returns the
parcel/reset without payout. Continue after delivery leaves you where you arrived.
C buys the existing 400-Credit cargo liner from Dispatch. Close the app to reset
the session. F3 bookmarks a suspicious contact or combo; F2 shows cargo receipts.

Try these five lines, then repeat your favorite without following the suggestion:

1. **Depot / Style:** make one deliberate fast Drive drift in the open yard.
   The stronger, warmer trail means qualifying slip; thin straight trails still
   reflect ordinary BRRR. Earn 3.5 continuous seconds above 22 m/s with 15–75° slip.
   Straightening, slowing, unfolding or wall contact ends the current combo.
   A completed combo remains earned. There is no grace timer in this candidate.
2. **Relay / Commitment:** organize the craft before the western outer sweep.
   Earn one uninterrupted 170 m Drive line above 24 m/s, at no more than 45° slip.
   The short interior route still delivers, but did not earn the bonus in testing.
3. **Works / Care:** read the existing service bar, unfold early and Hop, or take
   a bypass. Arrive with at least 85% cargo. The accepted cargo model is unchanged.
4. **Clinic / Thread:** take the southern approach through Depot. Cross the South
   Cut from west to east in Spread, using the existing Hop bar or the dogleg.
   Stay clean through that feature; no tiny slots or new obstacles were added.
5. **Quarry / Haul:** travel through Clinic to East Sweep, north toward Relay,
   then west over Quarry Shelf. Earn clean Drive 160 m → moving Spread 40 m →
   clean Drive 90 m. Finished phases stay earned; unfinished Drive fragments do
   not join across a broken line. This is the longest and least forgiving trial.

All endpoint deliveries pay base, including zero cargo and missed bonuses. Map
routes are advisory. Thread's crossing is an optional feature objective; Haul
observes a sequence, not geographical checkpoints. Creative legal alternatives
are valid. There is no new cosmetic reward, handling upgrade or permanent save.

Please judge: does drift feel distinct from speed, and does the trail explain it?
Does Relay ask for commitment, Works for an early unfold, and the south/Shelf for
new learning? Are five choices useful? Which line would you replay voluntarily?
In particular, report whether zero drift grace feels brittle, whether Thread is
too gentle, and whether Haul's second Drive leg feels readable or overly narrow.

Human feel, comfort, controller play and final balance remain unpassed owner gates.
