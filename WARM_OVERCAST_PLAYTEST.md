# Warm Overcast — owner playtest

Double-click PLAY_WARM_OVERCAST.command, or run `python3 tools/launch.py`.
Run v0 starts at Quarry. W/S thrust/brake; A/D steer; hold Shift for Drive;
Space for supported Spread Hop; R retry; Escape pause. F on the results card
returns to free roam. Tab opens the diagnostic location selector.

1. Look toward the Relay mast and Works stacks from approaching roads. Do the
   cool clouds and light horizon separate their silhouettes from the sky?
2. Cross each new yard in Spread and Drive. Is the drizzle visible without
   obscuring your line or feeling like speed streaks attached to the camera?
3. Compare damp paving with mineral ground. Are edges and obstacles still clear?
4. Pause and retry once, then complete a line of your choice to Relay. Does rain
   resume cleanly, and does the orange map marker agree with Relay's position?
5. Compare the new survey with the previous warm daylight version. Would you
   keep the overcast mood, lighten/darken it, or adjust only the drizzle?

Weather is cosmetic: handling, Hop, world geometry and BRRR are unchanged.
No puddles, splashes, lens effects or new audio. Existing rough R0 remains rough.
Comfort, preference and physical-gamepad judgments are Charlie's to make.
