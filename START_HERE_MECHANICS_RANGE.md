# Hush Basin — Mechanics Range v1

Double-click **PLAY_MECHANICS_RANGE.command**, or run
`python3 tools/play_alpha_loop.py` with exact installed Godot
`4.7.1.stable.official.a13da4feb`. Start at Market; E/Enter opens Dispatch,
1/2/3 selects, Enter accepts. Clickable cards and the existing B-button action
also remain available. No production deployment or main update.

| Job | Try this | Fixed reward |
|---|---|---|
| Depot / Freight seals | Give the open yard a deliberate drift, then settle | 80 base +60 for 100 slip BRRR |
| Relay / Longline coil | Set up the west sweep early and hold a Drive line | 100 base +200 for one 6-second BRRR streak |
| Works / Bench instruments | Read the service crossing; unfold early, Hop over it or take the side-pass | 120 base +35 at 85% cargo |

Slip BRRR is the slip contribution already present in the unchanged BRRR
formula. Straight acceleration contributes almost none to this objective.
The short twin ribbons widen and warm slightly during qualifying slip; they
remain faint and narrow during straight qualifying Drive. They are feedback,
not a new score formula. No purchase is needed for them.

For Relay, follow the advisory western map line toward Depot/West Gate and then
north on the outer sweep. The existing outside wall asks for early organization;
no new Relay hazard was added. The interior route still delivers and pays base.
A deliberate novel line is valid too: the bonus observes the streak, not which
road you used. There are no checkpoints or route-compliance penalties.

For Works, a single low service conduit crosses part of Works Way. Amber face
bands and an advance pavement label announce it. Unfold before reaching it;
on the direct line, a supported Hop about 8–10 m before the bar can clear it.
There is room to pass its end in Spread, and the northern route remains open.
A hot strike may spend the Care bonus. If stopped against it, unfold, back off
if needed and Hop or steer around; the parcel is still deliverable.

Movement is unchanged: W/S thrust/brake, A/D steer, Shift Drive/release Spread,
Space Hop. Escape pauses; R resets/returns an active parcel without payout.
Arrive supported below 6 m/s to deliver. Continue leaves you at the destination.
Drive back to Market's warm rooftop crown for another job.

Missing any bonus is fine; even zero-condition cargo pays base. Credits and the
400-Credit, 25% cargo liner remain session-only. No handling upgrades. F2 keeps
cargo receipts available and F3 bookmarks a surprising contact; local logs are
in `review_logs/alpha-*.jsonl`. Accepted cargo tuning was not reopened.

## Owner play

Play freely for a few deliveries. Try the easy line once, then deliberately
attempt the bonus. Try Works both hot and with an early unfold, and Relay both
inside and outside. These are mechanics experiments, not final missions.

1. Does Depot make you deliberately drift instead of simply hold Drive?
2. Does the Relay bonus make the outer sweep an appealing choice?
3. Does Works make you unfold because you read the approaching feature?
4. Are easy legal routes still acceptable when you do not want the bonus?
5. Do the ribbons explain slip without demanding HUD attention?
6. Does the conduit feel like road language rather than an arbitrary obstacle?
7. Do you want to improve a line rather than just collect Credits?

See `MECHANICS_CALIBRATION.md` for successful and failed trials and provisional
targets; `MECHANICS_RESULTS.md` for exact checks, cost and build-size movement.
