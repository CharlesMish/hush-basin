# Working Neighborhood — Charlie's playtest

Launch PLAY_WORKING_NEIGHBORHOOD.command. W/S thrust/brake; A/D steer;
hold Shift for Drive, release for Spread; Space for supported Hop.
R retries, Escape pauses; F on the results card returns to free roam.

1. Approach Market, Clinic, Depot and Works from the road. Can you tell what
   each place does before reading the small signs?
2. Look along the foundation faces. Do the closed service bays and compound
   entrances feel grounded and believable? They are façades, not playable doors.
3. Pass through all three yards in both forms. Does the architecture add a
   sense of place while leaving your lines and shortcuts open?
4. Notice the repair patches, drains, roof equipment and occasional warm
   windows. Which details help, and which feel repetitive or too prominent?
5. Finish a route to Relay, retry once and return to free roam. Watch for rain
   entering roofs, distracting shimmer, unreadable obstacles or awkward contact.

Prefer: keep / quieter detail / stronger distinction between buildings.
Any place that felt convincing? Any place that still felt like a blockout?
Comfort, authenticity, visual preference and physical-gamepad feel remain yours
to judge. Handling, weather and Run rules have not been retuned.
