# First Courier Slice v1 — Market to Depot

Charlie's September 15, 2026 commission authorizes one gameplay successor of
accepted World Identity / Detail v2 commit 0e995643c36d32e4ce03ef0d1b3b390949080480.
It supersedes historical no-gameplay freezes only for the courier sibling,
its new main scene, terse UI, diagnostic export entry and successor tests.
The retained P1B study is research, not implementation authority, and stays
byte-identical. Its three-job catalog, economy, paints and progression are out.

One Market → Depot parcel. Resolve both inner pads from authoritative world
data. Start free roam at Market, require a fresh Dispatch and Accept, allow any
route, require a supported controlled arrival, freeze results, then Continue
at the exact reached transform/velocity. No success teleport. No timeout, pace
penalty, grade, Credits, persistence, additional contracts or production deploy.
Run v0 remains the unchanged separate scene and launcher. BRRR uses its existing
pure seed unchanged, with no connection to condition or rewards.

Keep all movement, tuning, collider/probes, craft/Quarto, camera, world, weather,
surface classifications and Run code byte-identical. Courier observes after
movement at physics priority 200. It may pause for modals and use existing
spawn/reset APIs at initial scene entry and normal reset recovery only.

Cargo: fresh impact-counter increments plus the same completed slide's native
contact data. Exclude terrain and support-facing/mixed ambiguous contacts;
never infer damage from speed, slip, braking, transformation, Hop or landing.
Charge only an episode's rising severity peak. Continuous obstacle contact
keeps an episode open even when it produces no new strong impact. Zero condition
still delivers. Validate this seam in native physics before shipping a candidate;
stop rather than change movement semantics if clean/strike separation fails.

Pause/modal time is excluded. Reset, fall reset or diagnostic relocation cancels
the active parcel once, before any delivery check; it cannot award a delivery.
Results are immutable. Modal exits wait for neutral inputs and no physical edge
may both dismiss a modal and move/accept another job. Changing a recovery anchor
after Continue does not move the player.

Use exact installed Godot 4.7.1.stable.official.a13da4feb, native Forward+ and
the established local Web export/smoke lane. No new dependencies/assets or
hosting changes. Preserve old manifests and record superseded hash checks.
After complete verification, at most two bounded repair rounds. Human enjoyment,
cargo fairness, arrival satisfaction and flow remain Charlie's review gates.
