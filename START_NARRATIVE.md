# Hush Basin — Narrative Presence Lab v0.1

Double-click **PLAY_NARRATIVE.command** on macOS, or run
`python3 tools/play_narrative.py` from this folder. It uses the already-installed
Godot 4.7.1; nothing is installed or downloaded.

For a fresh play, open Dispatch at Market and choose **Reset Narrative
Experiment…**, then confirm. This resets the experiment, not your older Relay save.

W/S thrust/brake · A/D steer · hold Shift for Drive · Space for Hop.
E/Enter interacts. Enter advances, Left reviews the preceding line, X skips,
Escape closes or pauses. Work always needs a separate acceptance.

Play freely. There is no reading timer or deadline. You can leave an offer waiting.

After playing:

- Tell me what happened with those two.
- What do you think each of them wanted?
- Is there anything there you’d want to come back for?
