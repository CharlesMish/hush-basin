# Quiet Surfaces — Charlie's short playtest

Launch PLAY_QUIET_SURFACES.command. W/S thrust/brake, A/D steer; hold Shift
for Drive and release for Spread. Space is supported Hop. R retries,
Escape pauses, and F on the results card returns to free roam.

1. Drive both sweeps. Do the road edges feel cleaner without becoming distracting?
2. Cross the Market courtyard by an improvised line. Is it still clearly open?
3. Visit Quarry Shelf. Does the mineral ground belong with the district?
4. Look for a few repaired road patches and flat service covers near buildings.
   They are markings only. Does any one look like a physical obstacle or hole?
5. Retry, then return to free roam. Watch for sparkle, shimmer or blurred road
   edges while steering and braking.

Keep / quieter surfaces / stronger material distinction?
Where does the ground feel convincing? Where does it still feel unfinished?
Comfort, visual preference, authenticity and physical-gamepad feel remain yours
to judge. Terrain shape, handling, weather and Run rules have not been retuned.
